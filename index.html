02 — OUR STORY

Two journeys.
One UNICA.

Founded by Doula Al Shakankiry and Hagar El Kady, UNICA was born from a shared belief that every home should tell its own story.

Bringing together architecture, interior design, product design and Egyptian craftsmanship, UNICA creates furniture and objects with individuality at their core.
